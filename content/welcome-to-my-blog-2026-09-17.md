This is the first post on the new blog! I've wanted a place to write about engineering leadership, distributed systems, and building software. A big part of my life is working with people while building on different technologies. This blog will be a reflection of my thoughts on those, and will hold advice and suggestions for whoever finds them useful.

## What I'll write about

- Engineering leadership, strategic planning and working backwards from an end goal
- Building systems from scratch - like [kivo](https://itsmunim.github.io/kivo/), a redis-compatible memory store I wrote in Go
- Microservices patterns and distributed systems gotchas. My favorite part of it will be technologies like - Kubernetes, Kafka, Flink, ArgoCD, Loki etc.
- Hard-won lessons from operating large-scale consumer services
- The life struggles of engineering leaders - no one talks about
- My thoughts about AI, product architecture, design, and everything interesting

Let me know in the comments below if you would like to hear about something else. I look forward to seeing you around!
